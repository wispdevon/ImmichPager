# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors.

Report concerns privately through the repository's private reporting channel. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and disclose information only when needed to address the concern.
